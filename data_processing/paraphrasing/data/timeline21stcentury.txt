== [[2000s]] ==

===[[2001]]===
* [[January 15]]: [[Wikipedia]] was launched.
* [[January 20]]: 
** President [[Joseph Estrada]] of the [[Philippines]] is ousted in the [[Second EDSA Revolution]].
** [[George W. Bush]] is inaugurated as [[President of the United States]].
* [[January 26]]: An [[2001 Gujarat earthquake|earthquake]] strikes [[Gujarat]], [[India]], on [[Republic Day (India)|Republic Day]], resulting in more than 20,000 deaths.
* [[June 1]]: Eleven members of the royal family of [[Nepal]], including the king and queen, [[Nepalese royal massacre|are killed]] by Crown Prince [[Dipendra of Nepal|Dipendra]], who wounds himself and dies three days later.
* [[September 11]]: [[September 11 attacks]]: Nineteen [[Al-Qaeda]] terrorists hijack four planes, [[American Airlines Flight 11|crashing]] [[United Airlines Flight 175|two]] into the twin towers of the [[World Trade Center (1973–2001)|World Trade Center]] in [[New York City]], the [[American Airlines Flight 77|third]] plane into [[the Pentagon]] in [[Washington, DC]], while the [[United Airlines Flight 93|fourth]] plane is downed on the outskirts of [[Stonycreek Township, Somerset County, Pennsylvania|Stonycreek Township, Pennsylvania]]. 2,996 people, including 2,977 victims and 19 hijackers, died in the attacks.
* [[September 18]]: Start of [[2001 anthrax attacks|anthrax letter attacks]].
* [[October 7]]–[[December 17]]: The [[United States invasion of Afghanistan|United States invades Afghanistan]] and topples the [[Islamic Emirate of Afghanistan|Taliban regime]], resulting in a [[War in Afghanistan (2001–2021)|long-term war]].
* [[December 3]]: [[Enron]] files for Chapter 11 bankruptcy after [[Enron scandal|a series of scandals]].
* [[December 11]]: [[China and the World Trade Organization|China becomes]] a member of the [[World Trade Organization]].
* [[December 19]]–[[December 20|20]]: During an [[1998–2002 Argentine great depression|economic crisis in Argentina]], the government effectively [[Corralito|freezes all bank accounts]] for twelve months which [[December 2001 riots in Argentina|leads to riots]] and President [[Fernando de la Rúa]]'s resignation from office. There are five "presidents" in less than a month.
* [[Dean Kamen]] unveils [[Segway]].
* Media magnate [[Silvio Berlusconi]] was elected [[Prime Minister of Italy]].
{{see also|2001#Deaths}}

===[[2002]]===

* [[July 1]]: The [[International Criminal Court]] is established.
* [[July 9]]: The [[African Union]] is founded.
* [[September 19]]: The [[First Ivorian Civil War]] begins.
* [[October 12]]: The [[2002 Bali bombings]] killed 202 people and injured 209 more.
* [[October 23]]–[[October 26|26]]: Chechen rebels [[Moscow theater hostage crisis|seize a theater]] in Moscow. Amid this siege, around 200 people died.
* [[November 16]]: The [[2002-2004 SARS outbreak]] begins in [[Guangdong]], China.
* [[Switzerland]] joins the [[United Nations]] as the 190th member.
* [[Steve Fossett]] becomes the first person to fly solo nonstop around the world.
* [[2001–2002 India–Pakistan standoff]] ends.
* Israel starts [[Operation Defensive Shield]] in the West Bank in response to a wave of [[List of Palestinian suicide attacks#2000s|Palestinian suicide attacks]].
* Construction of the [[Israeli West Bank barrier]] begins.
* The [[Guantanamo Bay detention camp]] is established.
* [[Hu Jintao]] is elected as [[General Secretary of the Chinese Communist Party]].

===[[2003]]===

* The [[War in Darfur]] begins.
* The [[Human Genome Project]] is completed.
* The [[Second Congo War]] ends with more than 5 million dead.
* The [[Second Liberian Civil War]] ends.
* The last [[Volkswagen Beetle]] is made in Mexico, after 65 years in production.
* Final flight of the SST (Supersonic Transport) [[Concorde]].
* [[Capture of Saddam Hussein]].

* [[November 7]]–[[December 23]]: The [[Second Battle of Fallujah]] occurs. It is the deadliest American battle since the [[Vietnam War]], killing 95 troops.
* [[November 18]]: [[Massachusetts]] becomes the first [[U.S. state]] to [[Goodridge v. Department of Public Health|legalize]] [[same-sex marriage]].
* [[December 8]]: [[Union of South American Nations]] formed.
* [[December 26]]: [[2004 Indian Ocean earthquake and tsunami|Boxing Day Tsunami]] occurs in the [[Indian Ocean]], leading to the deaths of 230,000.
* [[Enlargement of NATO|NATO]] and [[2004 enlargement of the European Union|the European Union]] incorporates [[2004 enlargement of the European Union|most of the former Eastern Bloc]].
* [[War in North-West Pakistan]] begins.
* [[Spirit (rover)|Spirit]] and [[Opportunity (rover)|Opportunity]] land on [[Mars]]. 
* [[Orange Revolution]] in [[Ukraine]].
* First surface images of [[Saturn]]'s moon [[Titan (moon)|Titan]].
